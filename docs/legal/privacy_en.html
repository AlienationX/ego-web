Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: October 2026
Welcome to Ego Wallpaper! We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how Ego Wallpaper ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile applications and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a smooth wallpaper browsing, customization, and downloading experience, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Device and Network Information: We may collect non-sensitive technical data including your device model, operating system version, unique pseudonymous device identifiers (e.g., IDFV, Android ID, OAID), language settings, IP address, and network connection type to ensure app compatibility and network stability.
  • Usage and Interaction Data: We collect operational logs regarding your feature usage, search keywords, wallpaper view and download history, favorites, and performance crash logs to analyze stability and optimize user experience.
  • Account Information: If you choose to register or log in, we collect your nickname, email address, avatar, and login credentials.
  • Purchase and Subscription Data: When you purchase VIP memberships, transaction records (e.g., order ID, purchase time, subscription status) are processed through official app store platforms (e.g., Apple App Store In-App Purchase). We do not collect or store your sensitive payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and deliver wallpaper services, including personalized recommendations and downloads.
  • To manage your account, verify your VIP membership rights, and fulfill transactions.
  • To monitor application performance, diagnose technical errors, and enhance security and crash prevention.
  • To respond to your inquiries, customer support requests, and feedback.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

We may integrate trusted third-party software development kits (SDKs) and service providers to facilitate app functionality:

  • In-App Purchases: Apple StoreKit / In-App Purchase services for processing subscriptions securely.
  • Analytics and Crash Diagnostics: Crash reporting and statistical analytics tools used exclusively to track performance and fix bugs.
  • Advertising Services: For free users, third-party advertising partners may display non-personalized or contextual ads. VIP subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.

These third-party providers only access information necessary to perform their specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for other purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We employ standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions are encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR in the European Economic Area or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • The right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

You can exercise these rights directly within the app settings or by contacting our support team via email.
